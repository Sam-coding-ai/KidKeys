### 🚨 The Problem
Toddlers love imitating parents by smashing keyboards. One second of looking away turns into accidental email deletions, random gibberish documents typed out, closed browser tabs, or launching unintended apps. 

### 💡 The Solution
**KidKeys** is a lightweight, background Windows utility built specifically for parents. With a quick tap of a hotkey, it instantly locks down your entire keyboard, mouse clicks, and touchpad gestures so your child can safely watch a video or mash keys without wreaking havoc on your computer.

---

### ✨ Key Features

* **Instant Lockout:** Tap **CAPS LOCK 3 times** to completely freeze all keyboard input, mouse clicks, and touchpad gestures.
* **Smart Touchpad Shield:** Deep-level hardware disabling blocks Precision Touchpad multi-touch gestures completely.
* **Secure Emergency Unlock:** Tap **CAPS LOCK 5 times** to instantly restore your system. 
* **Zero Distractions:** Runs quietly in your **Windows System Tray** with a clean padlock icon.
* **Startup Ready:** Automatically configures to protect your machine right when Windows boots up.
* **Auditory Cues:** Subtle audio feedback lets you know when locking and unlocking are successful without popping up intrusive windows.
